Refunds & Recovery

What happens when a customer has paid but the provider cannot deliver. Orders where the provider definitely refused are refunded at once. Orders where the provider's state is unknown are never refunded or retried automatically: they wait in the review list. Temporary failures (maintenance, low provider balance) are retried on the schedule below, then refunded.

Maintenance

Switch a service off for new payments (customers see your message, never a provider name). Paid orders keep being recovered. Retries pause until the end time.

Provider Routing

Decide which processor Net365 tries first for each service. Customers never see this. Default: VTpass first for electricity, cable TV, education, data and insurance, with Reloadly on standby; Reloadly first for airtime. A provider you mark as down, or that Net365 senses is down (a run of recent failures), is moved to the back automatically and retried after a short cool-down.

Modes: Default = Net365's standard order. Auto = best 7-day score first. VTpass / Reloadly = force that processor first (the other stays on standby). Score = share of orders that succeeded in the last 7 days (starts at 50 until there is evidence).

Overseas Airtime: who serves each country

Each foreign country has exactly one processor, so the two never clash. Reloadly (default) is paid by card through Stripe in the destination currency. VTpass is paid in naira through Paystack and sells VTpass's own bundles and top-ups. Nigeria is not listed here: it follows the Airtime routing above. Countries not listed stay with Reloadly.

Ask Net365: customer questions

Every question typed into the Ask box, what Net365 answered, and whether the customer said it helped. When Net365 has no reliable answer it tells the customer you will get back to them, and the question lands here. Your reply appears in the Ask box if they are online, in their notification bell, and by email.

Membership numbers and Founders

A permanent number (N365-000042) is issued when a customer's email is verified, in that order. The number is the Founders rank: 1 to 100 Founders 100, 101 to 1,000 Pioneer 1,000, 1,001 to 10,000 Early Adopter 10,000. Numbers are never reused. Existing verified customers were numbered by sign-up order when this was installed.
